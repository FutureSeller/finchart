- Three more candle derivations in the Renko lineage, each `OHLC[] → …` on an ordinal x with `closedAt`, registered with `derive` so a tick goes in as a candle: `lineBreak(source, { lines })` (`LINE_BREAK_DEFAULTS`, Nison's three-line break — a reversal beyond the extreme of the last `lines` lines, opening at the last line's open), `kagi(source, { reversal })` (points with `tone` yang/yin and `breakY` where the line crosses the previous shoulder or waist; the last point is the live end) and `pointAndFigure(source, { boxSize, reversal })` (`POINT_AND_FIGURE_DEFAULTS`, columns of X's and O's on an absolute grid of the box's multiples — integer levels priced by one multiplication, a decimal tie snapped within a relative 8 × 2⁻⁵²; a level 2⁴⁷ boxes or more from zero, a box that is not a normal double, or a price past the doubles is refused).
- Two series to draw them: `kagiSeries()` (a stepped line at two widths, `--chart-kagi-up-width` / `--chart-kagi-down-width`, colours the candle's; M4 decimation at four per pixel, declared) and `pointAndFigureSeries({ boxSize })` (an X as two strokes, an O as a ring, `--chart-pnf-width`; only the boxes inside the pane, a run of cells under 4 px as one bar, assuming of the value axis only that it is monotone; the data door holds each column's ends to the box's grid and its `open`/`close` to its `tone`; `PnfAggregation` merges columns at one per pixel), with `KAGI_STYLE_SPEC` and `POINT_AND_FIGURE_STYLE_SPEC` public.
- `atrPriceStep(source, { period })` — the last Wilder ATR of a tape as a price distance, the one step for `renko`'s `brickSize`, `kagi`'s `reversal` and `pointAndFigure`'s `boxSize`; it refuses fewer bars than `period` and an ATR that is no step (0, subnormal, or over half the largest double), so a transform never fails under a name that is not its own.
- `renko` refuses a close 2⁴⁷ bricks or more from zero (past that a brick is under the doubles' spacing and the brick loop could not end) and a brick over half the largest double (its two-brick reversal would not fit the doubles).
- The guide's "Price-axis transforms" section is the recipe (derive + `updateLast`, `closedAt` as the axis label, what does not fit the lineage), and the `price-axis-transforms` example page shows all four under one ticking source; the `renko` example page moved there.
- Core: the value axis keeps its pixels and its fits finite at the edge of the doubles — a linear or log domain (or range) whose span overflows, a log domain of two adjacent doubles or one reaching from the smallest subnormal to the largest double, a fit whose padding would leave the doubles, ticks on any of those (finite, inside the domain, ascending, spaced) — and `CoordinateAccessor.getPositiveFloor?(point)` lets a point name the smallest positive value it holds, which a log axis fitted over a point that dips to zero or below stands on (a Point & Figure column crossing zero stands on its lowest box above it; candles and lines are unchanged).
