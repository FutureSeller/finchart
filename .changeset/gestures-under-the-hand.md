`paneMaximize`'s Esc and double-click sit below the default input priority, so a drawing in progress or under the cursor answers first wherever the tools were installed — Esc cancels the line being drawn before it restores the panes, and double-clicking a drawn line selects it instead of maximizing its pane. Under `wheel: "modifier"`, a sideways trackpad swipe still pans — the setting decides when the wheel zooms, and a swipe left to the page became the browser's back gesture. And `bandSeries` describes its point as its upper and lower edge (`U`, `L`), so a band registered with a name reads as the range instead of its upper edge alone.
