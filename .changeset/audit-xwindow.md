When a series draws a bar body — candles, OHLC bars, histograms, point-and-figure columns — a fit pads each end of the x window by half a bar (half the gap to the neighbouring bar of those series, half an index under `barIndexX`), so the first and last bodies are drawn whole instead of cut down the middle by the plot edge; a chart of lines, areas and baselines alone still fits edge to edge. A custom series opts in with the new `Series.barBody`. `rightOffset` adds on top, and `scrollToRealTime` and `shiftVisibleRangeOnNewBar` treat that padded end as the live edge. A chart mounted empty and fed bar by bar fills the screen only until its bars would be narrower than the default bar spacing (8px); from there it keeps that width and follows the newest bar until the window is moved. A lone bar gets a window ten bars wide (at the spacing the chart last knew, or one x unit per bar) instead of ±5% of its timestamp. New `plot.leadingMargin()` reports the half bar a fit leaves before the first point, in data x (`0` with no bar-bodied series); `infiniteHistory` reads it (it is now part of `InfiniteHistoryHost`), so a plain fit no longer reads as missing history and fetches a page.
