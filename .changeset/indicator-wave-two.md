- Eleven indicators from the KLineChart canon, each on the fold builder so a tick is one step, each with its `attach*` and, where it takes options, its `*_DEFAULTS`: `roc` (`{period, signal}`), `trix` (`{period, signal}`), `psy` (`{period, signal}`, with `PSY_LEVELS` 75/25 on a fixed 0–100 pane), `vr` (`{period, signal}`), `bbi` (`{periods}` — exactly four sma windows, a price-pane overlay labelled `BBI(3,6,12,24)`), `dma` (`{fast, slow, signal}`), `cr` (`{period, periods}` — the band and four averages drawn `ceil(p / 2.5 + 1)` bars back, five lines on an own pane with a line at 100), `brar` (`{period}` → `{br, ar}`), `emv` (`{period}` → `{emv, signal}`), `pvt` (no options → `{pvt}`), `kdj` (`{period, smooth, signal}` → `{k, d, j}`, with `KDJ_LEVELS` 80/20 on an autoScale pane). Where a formula is undefined the reading is `null`, never the canonical's 0 — the README's "where the readings part from KLineChart" says where, and why EMV takes one option, BRAR clips BR only, CR sums over the window and KDJ is not a `stochastic` option.
- `FourPeriods` — the tuple type BBI and CR take; a JavaScript caller's array of any other length is refused with a `ContractError` naming the factory.
- Two more fold kernels: `seededRmaFold(period, seed)` (a Wilder recursion that starts at a constant and applies the recurrence from the first observation on; a null input keeps the state) and `smaFold` rebuilt as `sumFold / period` — the window summed afresh each step instead of a running total. The running total could be poisoned for good by finite inputs (two 1e308 bars left a signal window at Infinity) and carried a departing outlier's residual; over a short tape the new form agrees with the old to a few ulp (13 at most on a 400-bar tape at period 20), over a long one it parts from the old by exactly the old total's accumulated drift, staying within the window's own rounding of the exact mean regardless of history. `SmaState` is now the sum fold's state — treat it opaquely, as documented.
- `FoldSpec.headLookback` is optional: a fold whose memory is not a bar count — a running sum (PVT), a recursion that skips null inputs (KDJ) — declares no head door and lands a prepended page by recomputing, the way `obv` and `vwap` do. The README matrix shows `—` in the head column for those.
- The numeric contract every fold follows is stated once, on `FoldSpec.step`: a value that is not finite, or that saturates at the largest double, is no observation and no state (a window with such a value is null; a recursion whose step leaves the range skips it and keeps its state); rounding, gradual underflow included, is the reading; a formula's undefined cases are decided on its inputs.
- Migration: nothing to change. On ordinary tapes `sma`-based readings move by a few ulp (see above); where the old running total had gone wrong — the residual a departing outlier left behind, a sum a finite overflow had poisoned for good — they now read the window's mean, a correction rather than a rounding. `SmaState`'s shape changed (it was documented as opaque): a snapshot taken by this version does not restore into an older one; an older snapshot restores here, its surplus field ignored.
